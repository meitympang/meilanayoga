Your first class

What to bring

Comfortable clothes and some water. Mats and props are provided, but you're welcome to bring your own.

Arrive early

Come 10 minutes before your first class so we can say hello and talk about any injuries.

Go at your pace

Every pose has options. Rest whenever you need to. There's no competing and no judgement.
