Before you come

Good to know

  • Wear comfortable clothes you can move in. We practise barefoot.
  • Mats, blocks, straps and bolsters are provided.
  • Please arrive 10 minutes early for your first class.
  • Let me know about any injuries, health conditions or pregnancy before class.
  • Avoid a heavy meal in the two hours before.
Booking & cancelling

Simple and flexible

  • Book online through Bookwhen. You'll get a confirmation email and a reminder.
  • Cancel up to 12 hours before and your class goes back on your pass.
  • Classes are capped at [12] so everyone gets attention.
  • Can't find a space? Join the waiting list on Bookwhen.
