Christmas Evening Flow & Mince Pies

Monday 21 December 2026 · 6:15 – 7:45pm · Melton Pavilion, Melton Recreation Ground, Melton Road, Melton, IP12 1PF · £7

Welcome to Meilana Yoga!

Take a proper breather from the December madness and join me for my very first class in Woodbridge.

We'll start with an easy-going evening flow to stretch out the winter stiffness and warm up, before slowing things right down with some cosy, restorative poses to help you switch off before Christmas.

Whether you've been doing yoga for years or you've never stepped on a mat before, you are so welcome. There's no pressure and no need to be flexible. Just come as you are.

To celebrate launching in Woodbridge, this first class is just £7, and afterwards I'll be putting the kettle on for some complimentary Christmas tea and mince pies for anyone who fancies a natter before heading home.

Good to know

Before you come

  • Bring with you: a yoga mat (drop me a message if you need to borrow one), a cosy jumper or thick socks for the relaxing bits at the end, and a bottle of water.
  • Getting there: Melton Pavilion is on Melton Recreation Ground, Melton Road, Melton, IP12 1PF, just outside Woodbridge. There's a car park at the Recreation Ground entrance, and an accessible path leads up to the Pavilion. Toilets are inside the building. About the Pavilion
