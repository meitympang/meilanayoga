Yoga built around you

A private session gives you my full attention. We'll work on what matters to you, at a time that suits you, in your home or online.

Who it's for

Good reasons to go private

Building confidence

Learn the basics in private before joining a group class.

Specific goals

Flexibility, strength, posture, better sleep or a home practice you'll stick to.

Working around injuries

A careful, adapted practice for niggles and stiffness. Please check with your GP or physio first.

Busy schedules

Early mornings, evenings or weekends, whenever works for you.

Parties & groups

Hen parties, birthdays, care homes and community groups. Gentle chair yoga or a fun flow, planned around your group.

Deepening practice

Refine poses, try new ones and explore breathwork and meditation.
