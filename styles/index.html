Yoga styles

Which style is right for you?

Each style moves at a different pace. All of them are open to beginners, and you can mix and match through the week.

● ○ ○  Gentle

Restorative Yoga

Long, fully supported holds and slow breathing. Calms the nervous system and helps with stress and sleep.

Beginners Hatha Yoga

Energising Vinyasa Yoga
